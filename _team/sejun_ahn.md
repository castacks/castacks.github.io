Sejun Ahn is a visitor at the Robotics Institute, Carnegie Mellon University. His research interests include learning-based inertial odometry and robust state estimation.

He is also pursuing an integrated M.S.-Ph.D. degree in Mechanical and Robotics Engineering at the Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST), where he is affiliated with the Machine Perception and Intelligence Lab (MPIL).
