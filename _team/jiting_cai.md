Jiting Cai is a Master's student at Carnegie Mellon University. Jiting's research interests lie at the intersection of computer vision, robotics, and multimodal learning, with a focus on robot perception, including simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM), multimodal representation learning, and world models for robotics.

Previously, Jiting worked on visual reasoning, physical reasoning, simulated scene generation, and robot manipulation. Jiting is currently working on key robot perception problems, including correspondence, multimodal representation learning, and SLAM.
