Xiang (Shawn) Fei is a PhD student at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by [Prof. Sebastian Scherer](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=gxoPfIYAAAAJ&hl=de) at the AirLab. Prior to that, he also earned his M.S. degree in Robotics from CMU [Robotics Institute](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/) in 2026, advised by [Prof. Howie Choset](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=4fvo61oAAAAJ&hl=en) at the Biorobotics Lab. Before joining CMU, he received his Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering from the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHKSZ) in 2024.

Shawn's research aims at learned, robust, and generalizable spatial perception for robotics.
