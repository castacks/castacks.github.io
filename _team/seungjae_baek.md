Seungjae Baek is a PhD student at Robotics Institute, Carnegie Mellon University. He graduated from Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology (UNIST) in South Korea with B.S. in Electrical Engineering and M.S. in Artificial Intelligence. 

His research interests are robot learning, informative path planning, and multi-robot systems, with an emphasis on robust autonomy in real-world environments.
