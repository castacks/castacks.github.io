Seungjae Baek is a Ph.D. student at the Robotics Institute, Carnegie Mellon University. He earned his B.S. in Electrical Engineering and M.S. in Artificial Intelligence from Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology (UNIST) in South Korea.

His research interests are robot learning, informative path planning, and multi-robot systems, with an emphasis on robust autonomy in real-world environments.
