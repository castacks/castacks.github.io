Trevor is a Master’s student in Robotics at Carnegie Mellon University. He graduated from Colorado State University with a B.S. in Computer Science, concentrating in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence.

His research interests include learning-based control, model-based reinforcement learning, and online adaptation for autonomous mobile robots. At the AirLab, he is working on learning-based adaptive dynamics models to enable autonomous off road driving across changing terrain and vehicle platforms.
