Ore Abolade is an M.S. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Sebastian Scherer. He completed his B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University, with a minor in Artificial Intelligence. His work began in computer vision and robotic perception, with a focus on multimodal sensing and depth estimation for robust autonomy. More recently, his research interests have expanded toward physical AI and robotic reasoning, with the goal of building general-purpose robots that can understand and act in real-world environments.
