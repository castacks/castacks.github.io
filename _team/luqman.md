Luqman Zaceria is a master's student in the MS in Computer Vision program at Carnegie Mellon University's Robotics Institute. His work focuses on building AI systems that perceive, learn, and act in the real world. At AirLab, he works on field autonomy with Wenshan Wang. In 2026, after leading the autonomy team, he took on the role of project lead for the CMU Moon Miners, the university's NASA Lunabotics team.
