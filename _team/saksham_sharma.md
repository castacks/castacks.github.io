Saksham is a Master’s student in Robotics at Carnegie Mellon University and a member of the AirLab. His research goals are to build robust autonomous systems for challenging, unstructured environments in a self supervised way. Before joining CMU, he worked at Skild AI and ARTPARK at the Indian Institute of Science, after earning his B.E. in Electronics and Communication Engineering from RVCE.

When the robots temporarily cooperate and play in the mud, he enjoys making music, making synth to make music, animations and cycling.
