<!-- Strike vs Guard (SVG) is a game between two identical drone teams: strikers try to reach a protected target while guards defend it, and a striker and guard that collide are both lost. The SVG ground controller is the part that actually flies them. It fuses motion-capture positions into one shared picture of the room, takes each drone's next move from the running scenario, the operator or a pilot's gamepad, and passes every command through a safety filter 20 times a second that nudges drones apart before they get too close. The same controller flies drones in Isaac Sim, in CMU's motion-capture flight room, or both in the same run. -->
