SSIO trains inertial odometry on unlabeled IMU data from normal operation — no costly ground truth.

<!-- SSIO is a self-supervision framework for learned inertial odometry. We show that a short-window IMU integration teacher can train a velocity model from scratch without velocity or position labels. The student learns from these short-window targets yet predicts long-horizon velocity changes more accurately than its teacher. A new embodiment requires no motion capture, RTK, or SLAM ground truth, only unlabeled IMU recorded on the platform during normal operation. Across six datasets spanning pedestrians, drones, quadrupeds, and a humanoid, SSIO performs comparable to a supervised AirIO baseline. -->
