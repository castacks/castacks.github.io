AM-Bench studies how policy, control, embodiment, and physics shape aerial manipulation success. The CoRL 2026 project brings together 12 tasks, four robot embodiments, configurable physical effects, and shared policy and controller interfaces. Explore the [project website](https://ambench.github.io/), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00641), and [code](https://github.com/ambench/ambench).
